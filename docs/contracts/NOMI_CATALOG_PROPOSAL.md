# Milo / Nomi catalog coordination proposal

Prepared from Nomi PR #1 commit `8ab330262e8500eee72efea45a1b029561c8a6ab` (`scripts/schema.sql`) and Milo PR #1 commit `6fad2fdab8f5a3c244fb008a157aa896104f9d61`. This is a proposal for review, not an agreement with another team. No message was sent and no shared structure was created.

| Concept | Milo current local data | Nomi proposed autonomous SQL | Proposed boundary / decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Supplier identity | UUID, name, email, phone, lead_days, notes | `suppliers`: org UUID, identifier, identity_key, aliases; unique org/identity_key | One organization-scoped supplier identity with explicit source-ID links. Reuse an existing identity only after review. Define identity_key without name/email auto-merge. Contact fields and lead time need separate ownership/per-location overrides. |
| Ingredient/article | `items`: ingredient/packaging/resale/consumable, reference unit, allergens | No equivalent ingredient master in inspected schema | Shared article identity is a candidate, not Nomi supplier_products. Agree minimum attributes and writer ownership before creating it. Milo retains recipe-specific properties. |
| Purchased supplier reference | `references`: supplier + item, packaging, pack count, content unit, catalog HT price, minimum packs | `supplier_products`: org/location/supplier/reference/unit/characteristics, unique on these fields | Link purchased reference to an ingredient/article explicitly. Different scope and identity keys prevent a blind table merge. Preserve both source UUIDs via source links when two existing IDs refer to one identity. |
| Observed price | Chosen supplier reference price, not a verified invoice price | `price_observations`: document/line/date, price, physical_quantity, reliability, packaging | Nomi remains source for invoice evidence. Milo consumes a permitted accepted observation with provenance; no automatic overwrite of catalog/recipe costs on an unconfirmed OCR result. |
| Matching | Explicit chosen cost_reference_id | `product_mapping_decisions` per document with reason and actor | Matching must be reviewable/reversible; no inference that recipe ingredient = extracted product by label. |
| Cost/recipe | Recipes, portions, losses, labor, snapshots | No corresponding recipe calculation model | Milo owns recipe costs. Pulp may later receive a dated cost projection, not unrestricted catalog access. |

Proposed sources of truth: shared identity service for supplier/article identity; Milo for recipes and cost calculations; Nomi (`alerte_achats`, not `nomi`) for document evidence and observations. All shared reads require explicit organization/location/product permissions. Buying either product does not grant access to the other's private documents, recipes or financial history. No Helly subscription requirement.

Open decisions: physical table/schema names; organization-level identity visibility vs per-location commercial attributes; who may edit shared fields; least-privilege projection fields; merge/unmerge and source-ID uniqueness; acceptance workflow for reliable observations; event/outbox version and idempotency key (proposed Nomi observation UUID + version); financial permissions and post-expiration reading. Avoid two independent supplier masters while these are unresolved.

Conventions: UUIDs retained, English snake_case, NUMERIC monetary/quantity storage, EUR explicit in current Milo exports (currency conversion not supported), HT purchase costs vs TTC selling price, VAT explicitly recorded, timestamps UTC, invoice/operational dates in establishment timezone. Nomi normalizes kg/l/piece while Milo supports g/ml as compatible input conversions. Preserve original purchase packaging and its scale; never convert kg to litres without an item-specific validated conversion.

Future exchange: Nomi publishes an accepted observation through an authorized projection or transactional outbox; Milo deduplicates by source observation/version, records provenance and updates current cost only under an explicit rule. Old snapshots/inventories/production costs never change retroactively. Supplier identity creation/merge is a centralized business operation, not two independent upserts keyed by name.
